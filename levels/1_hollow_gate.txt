name: The Hollow Gate
subtitle: where the light runs out
biome: gate
hint: A / D to move.  Space to jump - hold it to jump higher.
hint: Spikes bite. You'll be thrown back to safe ground.
hint: Hold Left Mouse (or J) to draw your bow - release to fire. A full draw flies flat and hits harder.
hint: Shift to dash, even in mid-air. Jump first, then dash across.
hint: Shoot the target to open the gate.
hint: Arrows stick in wood. Shoot the wooden wall, then stand on your arrows to climb. (Only 3 stay stuck.)
hint: Right-click (or F) to swipe up close. Down + swipe in the air bounces you off foes and spikes.
hint: Crumbling stone falls away soon after you step on it - keep moving.
hint: S + Space drops you through wooden ledges.
