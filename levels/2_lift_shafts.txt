name: Lift Shafts
subtitle: the old miners went up, never down
biome: shafts
hint: An Arrow Shrine. Walk into it to take what it holds.
hint: Point at the floor by your feet and fire a Lift Arrow - the platform carries you up. (1-6 or Q/E switch arrows.)
hint: Lift Arrows also work on walls: a ledge sprouts and climbs. Shoot the far wall, then jump on.
hint: Special arrows cost a charge (the orbs). They refill over time - faster when you hit foes with steel.
hint: Two lifts can exist at once. Stack them.
